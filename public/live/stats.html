Overs lost (rain / bad light)

When play stops and overs are lost, enter the new total overs for the innings being played. Before the first ball it simply sets the innings length; mid-innings it records the score, wickets and overs at the stoppage for DLS.

Settings & manual overrides

A manual target always wins. Use it for the officials' Professional Edition target; the par score then still comes from the table unless you override it too.

Resource table

The built-in table is an approximation of the Standard Edition generated from the published D/L model. Paste the official Standard Edition table (51 rows: 0–50 overs left, 10 columns: 0–9 wickets lost) for exact Standard Edition figures.
